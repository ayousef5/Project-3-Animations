### Artist Statement

For this project, I wanted to see how I could make a simple grid of squares move in different ways. I started with the grid from my previous project, which had large black squares and smaller outlined squares. I kept the same pattern but experimented with different types of movement.

In Version 1, the black squares grow and shrink in a wave, while the outlined squares stay in place. In Version 2, the squares rotate independently at different speeds and directions. In Version 3, the shapes slowly drift around their original positions using noise. I also added three colors and fading trails to make the movement more interesting.

One artist who connects to my project is John Whitney Sr. His work *Matrix I–III* (1971–72) uses repeated shapes that move at different but related speeds. I liked this idea because I also used a repeated grid where each shape can have its own movement. His work helped me think about how simple shapes and movement can create interesting patterns over time.
